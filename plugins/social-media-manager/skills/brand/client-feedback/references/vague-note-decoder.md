# Vague-note decoder

A vague note is a real reaction without a clear instruction. Don't guess and act. Give 2–3 concrete `interpretations` (most likely first) and **one** short question that lets the client pick.

## 1. Three decoding questions

1. **Where?** A timestamp, scene, shot, poster area (headline, photo, offer box, footer) or "the whole thing".
2. **Which axis?**

   | Axis | Video | Poster |
   |---|---|---|
   | Message | offer, claim, script lines | headline, offer text |
   | Visual | shots, people, location, product | photo, illustration |
   | Colour | grade, brightness | palette, background |
   | Type | captions, on-screen text size and style | font, size, hierarchy |
   | Pace | cut speed, length, order | (layout density) |
   | Sound | music, voice-over, volume | — |
   | Brand | logo, colours, contact details | logo, colours, contact details |

3. **Compared to what?** A reference they liked, the last version, or a competitor.

The question asks the client for the missing piece, ideally as a choice: "A ya B?" WhatsApp clients answer choices faster than open questions.

## 2. Common phrases

Likely meanings, most likely first. Adjust them to what was shown.

| They say | Likely means (video / poster) | Question to send |
|---|---|---|
| "thoda aur attractive banao" / "make it more attractive" | (1) brighter, more colourful look; (2) a stronger first 2 seconds or headline; (3) better-looking people or product shots | "Attractive matlab: colours zyada bright, shuru ka hook zyada strong, ya photos/models better?" |
| "premium feel chahiye" / "look premium" | (1) fewer elements, more empty space; (2) darker or richer palette (black, gold, deep colours); (3) slower, calmer music and cuts | "Premium ke liye: kam text aur saaf design, ya dark-gold rich colours, ya dono?" |
| "kuch missing hai" / "something's missing" | (1) the offer or price isn't clear enough; (2) no call to action or contact; (3) no product or shop shown | "Kya missing lag raha hai: offer, contact number, ya dukaan/product ka shot?" |
| "zyada bhara hua lag raha hai" / "too crowded" | (1) too much text; (2) too many elements; (3) the font is too big | "Kya hatayein: kuch text, ya kuch photos/icons?" |
| "pop karna chahiye" / "make it pop" | (1) higher contrast between text and background; (2) a bigger, bolder headline or offer; (3) brighter accent colour | "Pop ke liye: offer bada aur bold, ya colours zyada bright?" |
| "boring lag raha hai" / "it's boring" | (1) cuts too slow; (2) music too flat; (3) the opening doesn't hook | "Boring kahan laga: shuru mein, music mein, ya poori speed slow hai?" |
| "fast hai" / "too fast" | (1) cuts too quick to read text; (2) voice-over too fast; (3) the video is too short to take it in | "Fast: text padhne ka time kam hai, ya voice-over tez hai?" |
| "thoda simple rakho" / "keep it simple" | (1) fewer effects and transitions; (2) less text; (3) plainer colours | "Simple matlab: effects kam, text kam, ya dono?" |
| "local feel chahiye" / "apne sheher ka lage" | (1) city landmarks or areas named; (2) Hindi or Hinglish voice and words; (3) local faces | "Local ke liye: Indore ki jagah dikhayein, bhasha zyada desi, ya local log?" |
| "professional nahi lag raha" / "not professional" | (1) text alignment and fonts inconsistent; (2) low-quality images; (3) too casual a tone | "Professional ke liye: design zyada clean, photos better, ya language formal?" |
| "colour theek nahi hai" / "colours don't feel right" | (1) not the brand colours; (2) too dull or too bright; (3) clashing combination | "Brand colours (maroon-gold) use karein, ya bas thoda bright/soft karna hai?" |
| "music change karo" (no detail) | (1) a different mood (calmer or more energetic); (2) a trending track; (3) quieter under the voice | "Kaisa music: soft, energetic, ya koi trending gaana?" |
| "logo dikhna chahiye" / "logo not visible" | (1) bigger logo; (2) logo earlier or longer on screen; (3) better contrast behind the logo | Usually concrete: label `in_scope` (bigger, longer) unless the placement is unclear. |
| "festive feel do" / "make it festive" | (1) festival decor and colours (diyas, marigold, lights); (2) festive music; (3) a greeting line | "Festive ke liye: diye/lights wala look, festive music, ya greeting line?" |
| "thoda emotional banao" | (1) a family moment or story; (2) softer music; (3) a warmer voice-over | "Emotional: family wala scene, ya music/voice soft?" |
| "wo wala jaisa" / "like that one" (reference unclear) | the reference they mean is missing | "Kaunsa wala? Link ya screenshot bhej dein." |
| "sab theek hai bas thoda aur better" | approval with an unspecified wish | "Agar ek cheez badalni ho to kya: colours, music, ya text?" (Treat the rest as approved.) |
| "dekh lo ek baar" / "check once" | they doubt something but don't say what | "Kis cheez pe doubt hai: spelling, number/price, ya photo?" |

## 3. Rules

- **Never send all interpretations as open questions.** One question per vague item, and at most 3 questions in one message. If more items need asking, put the most important first and ask the rest after.
- **A note that is clear enough to act on is `in_scope`,** even if it's informal ("logo bada karo" is concrete).
- **Words like "premium" or "attractive" that match the brand profile** (e.g. "premium" for a real-estate brand already described that way) can be answered from the profile. Say which interpretation the profile points to, but still ask.
- **Suggest a learning** when a vague note repeats for the same client ("Neha says 'premium' meaning fewer elements, twice now").
