# Plain brief card

About 6 lines, in the team member's language (Hinglish shown). Shown at the gate and in the reply, and safe to forward to the client. No jargon, no field names, no job-internal words beyond the job ID, and no phone numbers.

```markdown
**<JOB-ID> · <title>** (brief v<n>)
Kya: <offer in plain words, with exact price and last date — or the message if there's no offer>
Kiske liye: <who, where> (<age band>)
Log kya karein: <action + channel> (<one supporting line, e.g. free pickup>)
Kaisa: <length> <platform format> (+ <variants>) · <language> · <format in plain words>
Dhyan: <2-3 must-dos / must-avoids>
Guess kiya: <guessed fields that matter, or "kuch nahi"> · Pending: <open items with who, or "—">
```

## Rules

- **Lines in this order.** Leave a line out only if it's truly empty (e.g. "Kya" becomes the message for a brand film).
- **"Guess kiya" names only guesses that shape the work** (length, format, CTA). Never hide one to make the card look finished.
- **"Pending"** shows `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]` items and client questions still open, with who answers.
- **In English or Hindi:** What / For / They should / Format / Mind / Guessed / Pending, or क्या / किसके लिए / लोग क्या करें / कैसा / ध्यान / अंदाज़ा / बाकी.

## Example

```markdown
**SHL-03 · Winter blanket cleaning offer reel** (brief v2)
Kya: Kambal/razai dhulai ₹299 per piece, 30 Nov tak
Kiske liye: Vijay Nagar–Bhawarkua families (25-45)
Log kya karein: WhatsApp karein (free pickup ₹299 se upar)
Kaisa: 20 sec Insta reel + 15 sec WhatsApp status · Hinglish · offer card, koi chehra nahi
Dhyan: price pehle frame pe · captions on · "cheap" word nahi
Guess kiya: length 20 sec · Pending: —
```
