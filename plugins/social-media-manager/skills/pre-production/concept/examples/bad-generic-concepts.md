# Bad: generic concepts (annotated)

Same laundry brief as `good-laundry-offer-concepts.md` (SHL-04: ₹89/kg wash + iron, free pickup above ₹299, working couples in Vijay Nagar, 20 s, Hinglish).

```
SHL-04 concepts

Insight: Everyone wants clean clothes.                                   ← ✗ 1

C1 · Diwali Dhamaka — happy family in new clothes around the diyas,
     "Shubh Laundry wishes you a happy Diwali", logo at the end            ← ✗ 2
C2 · Indore's No.1 Laundry — "Trusted by 10,000+ customers! Best quality,
     lowest price! Hurry, offer ends soon!"                                ← ✗ 3
C3 · Laundry Made Easy — a model with a basket smiles at the camera,
     "Ab laundry ki tension khatam", price card at the end                 ← ✗ 4

Recommended: C2: most energetic.                                         ← ✗ 5
```

## What's wrong

1. **No insight, just a topic.** "Everyone wants clean clothes" is true of every laundry on earth; it can't choose between ideas. The real tension (they want their Sunday back; the press wala's timing runs their week) changes what the reel shows.
2. **The festive cliché, off-brief.** A diya-montage greeting (psychology §4) for an offer reel with no festival in the brief. No offer, no CTA, brand at the end, and a competitor could post the same thing tomorrow.
3. **Invented proof and fake urgency.** "10,000+ customers", "No.1", "lowest price" and "offer ends soon" aren't in the brief or profile. "Lowest price" also breaks the client's never-say-cheap rule. This is the misuse form of L01, L06 and L09.
4. **One idea in three costumes.** C1–C3 are all "offer + smiling people + logo"; same angle type, same format, same lever. A stock model instead of the real shop throws away the local-business advantage (T2, T7).
5. **A recommendation without a reason.** "Most energetic" isn't a criterion. No scores, no sources, no producibility, no risks.

## How the good version fixes each

| Problem | Fix (see the good example) |
|---|---|
| Topic as insight | Sourced tension: time, not price (research `F2`, `F7`) |
| Cliché | Real doorsteps and the real pickup bag; the research cliché (`F9`) flipped |
| Invented proof | Only ₹89/kg and ₹299 from the profile; gaps as `{{needed}}` |
| Same idea ×3 | offer/demo · story/pov · proof/before-after |
| No reason | Scored on 7 criteria; a recommendation with a one-line reason |
