## Why this is good

- **The insight is specific and sourced** (time, not price), and it changes the work: the reel sells the week, not the kilo.
- **Three real choices:** an offer demo, a story, a proof. Different formats and levers (value + convenience; immediate benefit + relatability; before/after + real-person voice).
- **Every number is theirs:** ₹89/kg and ₹299 come from the profile; nothing invented.
- **The research cliché was removed:** the white price card (`F9`) became a card *on the real bag*.
- **Producible tomorrow,** with no credits.
- **The hook beat is left open** for `hooks`, but the format makes a mute first frame possible.
