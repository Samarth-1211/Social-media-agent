## What makes C1 and C2 brand-film work

- **C1 uses SIT task unification** (`creative_method: sit-task-unification`): the RERA board, normally small print, becomes the opening title card and the film's proof. **Lever L15 (show the process) + L05 (specificity)**: the real date stamp on every monthly shot.
- **C2 uses bisociation** (moving home × a cricket team's first day in a dressing room: everyone has a place and a ritual). It plays on psychology T9 (family as a team, not the dining-table cliché). It needs a real family who agrees, or a clearly staged film with actors; never AI "residents". Difficulty med.
- **AI is used honestly:** C2's interiors of an unbuilt tower would be renders, labelled "AI-generated / representative" on screen; C1 needs no AI at all.
