# Angle engines

How to get 6–8 raw angles that are actually different, fast. Run the passes in order; stop at 8.

## Topic vs angle

- **A topic** is what the reel is about: "Diwali curtain cleaning offer".
- **An angle** is the specific way in: "the guest who notices the curtains before the sweets", "what really happens to your curtains inside our shop", "Diwali cleaning without losing a weekend".
- One topic has dozens of angles. A blank page is a missing-angle problem, not a missing-topic one.
- **Test:** if the line would fit any laundry in India, it's still a topic. Put in the thing only this client, this city or this customer can say.

## Angle types (label every angle)

| Type | Job | Typical proof |
|---|---|---|
| **proof** | Something that happened or can be seen, with evidence | Real footage, a real result, a process, a count the client gave |
| **teach** | One thing the viewer can use today | The owner's real know-how |
| **opinion** | A defensible position that some will disagree with | The owner's experience; a visible reason |
| **story** | A scene with a cost or a turn | A real customer (with consent) or a clearly staged scene |
| **offer** | What's sold, said plainly | The real price, date and conditions |

The three developed concepts must be **three different types**.

## Pass 1: what you already have

1. The brief's `downstream.concept.angle`.
2. Research `working_angles` (strong first).
3. A tension from psychology §3 that the brief's audience and offer fit.

## Pass 2: one topic, five angles

Take the topic and push it through five lenses, adapted for a local business:

| Lens | Question | Laundry example (fictional) |
|---|---|---|
| **Consumption** | What does the customer see, use or experience? | What a saree looks like when it comes back, in tissue |
| **Response** | What do people already say or believe that we can answer? | "Dry clean mein saree ka rang chala jaata hai" |
| **Curation** | What's the short list worth saving? | 3 fabrics you should never wash at home |
| **Instructional** | What can they do today? | How to check a dry cleaner in 30 seconds |
| **Narrative** | What happened, to whom, at what cost? | The wedding saree that came in a day before the shaadi |

## Pass 3: constraint fan-out

Constraints produce ideas; blank pages don't. Cross one item from each row and keep the combinations that spark:

| Constraint | Options to cross |
|---|---|
| **Pillar** (what the client is known for) | service quality · speed · price/value · the owner · the locality |
| **Format** (`concept-formats.md`) | POV · before/after · myth vs fact · founder story · customer story · process reveal · comparison · demo … |
| **Audience slice** (`audience_detail`) | working couple · student · homemaker · NRI son booking for parents · older customer |
| **Problem** (`pain_points`, `customer_words`) | no time · fear of damage · price doubt · trust in a new shop · festival pressure |

Example: *owner* × *myth vs fact* × *homemaker* × *fear of damage* → "Neha answers the three things homemakers fear about dry cleaning silk".

## Pass 4: brand films only

If `job_kind` is `brand-film`, add 1–2 angles with `creative-methods.md`. Not for offer reels: the methods are slower and the result is usually too abstract for a 20-second sale.

## Warm-up rule

The first two angles that come to mind are usually the category default. Write them down (they may still be right), then generate past them. At least one developed concept must come from Pass 2–4, not Pass 1.

## The hook test (before developing)

Try to write three different hooks for the angle in a minute. If all three feel flat, the angle is weak: remove it (`weak_hook`), don't polish it.

## Choosing the three

1. Remove what step 6 removes (competitors overdo, brief avoid, profile don't, lever misuse, weak hook).
2. Rank the rest on gut strength of insight + fit with the brief's objective.
3. Pick the top three that are **different angle types** and differ on at least two of: audience slice, format, lever, hook tactic.
4. The brief's angle doesn't have to be one of the three, but if it isn't, `recommended.reason` or a `why_not_higher` says why.
5. Keep the best remaining one as `backup`.
