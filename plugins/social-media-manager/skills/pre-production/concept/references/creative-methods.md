# Creative methods (brand films only)

For `job_kind: brand-film`: a longer, emotional piece where an unexpected idea is worth the extra thinking. Add **at most 2 angles** from here, then treat them like any other angle (score, filter, develop). Record the method in `creative_method`. Not for offer reels.

Work in the **closed world**: use only things already in the client's world (the product, the place, the people, the city, the customer's routine). Ideas built from these feel true and are cheap to shoot.

## SIT patterns

| Pattern | Move | Brand-film example (fictional, Jabalpur real estate) |
|---|---|---|
| **Subtraction** (`sit-subtraction`) | Remove something essential and show what's missing. | A film about a home with no walls shown at all: only the sounds of a family's day, until the last shot reveals the flat. |
| **Multiplication** (`sit-multiplication`) | Copy an element, with a change. | The same balcony, filmed at five ages of one family's life. |
| **Division** (`sit-division`) | Split the product or scene and rearrange it. | The walk from the gate to the door, cut into the five moments a parent worries about, each answered. |
| **Task unification** (`sit-task-unification`) | Give an existing element an extra job. | The RERA board at the site becomes the opening title card: proof as the hook. |
| **Attribute dependency** (`sit-attribute-dependency`) | Link two things that seem unrelated: the more X, the more Y. | The closer you get to possession day, the louder the sound of the Narmada in the film. |

## SCAMPER (`scamper`)

Ask each of these once about the obvious brand film, and keep the answer that surprises you:
Substitute (who tells it?), Combine (two audiences in one story?), Adapt (a format from another category?), Modify (make one detail huge?), Put to other use (where else is this place part of life?), Eliminate (drop the voice-over?), Reverse (tell it from the house's point of view?).

## TRIZ, two principles

- **Inversion** (`triz-inversion`): do the opposite of the category. Real estate films sell luxury; invert it and sell the ordinary Tuesday evening at home.
- **Extraction** (`triz-extraction`): pull one attribute out and make it the hero. Not "the project": just the morning light in the kitchen.

## Bisociation (`bisociation`)

Collide the client's world with an unrelated one and find the point they share.
Example: a family moving house × a cricket team's dressing room. Shared point: everyone has a fixed place and a ritual. Film: the first evening in the new home, as the "first innings".

## Rules

- Start from the core insight; a method is a way to express it, not a replacement.
- Must still be producible with the budget band (`producibility.md`). A concept that only works with heavy AI continuity is `high` difficulty: say so.
- If it needs explaining in more than one sentence, it's not ready: simplify or drop it.
