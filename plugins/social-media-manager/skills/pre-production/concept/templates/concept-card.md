# Concept card

The readable form of one item in `concept.data.concepts[]`. Shown in full at the creative pick (hooks) and on request ("concept 2 poora dikhao"). Write it in the person's language; keep each line short.

```
C<n> · <title>                                        <weighted>/50  [⭐ recommended]
<logline: one line, who wants what, what stands in the way, what changes>

Insight: <one sentence>  (source: <research id | brief field | profile field | assumption>)
Angle: <proof | teach | opinion | story | offer>  ·  Format: <format>  ·  Mechanic: <mechanic or —>
Levers: <L0x name> — proof: <real fact + where from | {{needed: …}}>
        <L0y name> — proof: <…>

Story (<duration_sec> s):
  0–2   <beat>: <what we see> / <what we hear>
  2–6   <beat>: …
  …     cta: <the brief's CTA, once>

Make it: <shot → source, …>
  Difficulty <low|med|high> · Credits <none|low|medium|high> · Needs from client: <list or —>
Risks: <kind: note → mitigation>
Why this client: <brand, language, city>
Why not a 5: <the one biggest weakness>
Scores: brief <n> · insight <n> · distinct <n> · India <n> · brand <n> · make <n> · risk <n>
```

Rules:
- Every number, price, date or customer on the card is real (brief, profile, research) or a visible `{{needed: …}}`.
- The story beats add up to `duration_sec`; the hook beat is the first 2–3 seconds and is left open for `hooks` to fill.
- Use the client's words and area names; no clichés from psychology §4 unless the card says how it flips them.
