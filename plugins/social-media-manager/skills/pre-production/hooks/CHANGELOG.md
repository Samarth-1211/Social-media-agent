# Changelog: hooks

## Unreleased (branch feat/concept-hooks, released with the plugin at merge)

- First version. Runs the `hooks` stage (quick and full video tracks, editor gate `creative-pick`): 8–10 three-layer hooks per concept (or per reused format on the quick track) with at least 5 formulas, 2 visual-led, 1 proof and 1 customer-phrase hook; 7-criterion weighted scoring (max 55) with a weakest-link cap and a learnings adjustment (±5); a critique pass on the 3 weakest; top 3 per concept; one recommended pair; self-check HC1–HC10.
- The creative pick: one compact message, natural replies ("2B", "1 with 3A", "mix 1 and 3", "more like 2A", "none, …", line edits), optional auto-pick (`creative_auto_pick_after_hours`, off by default) logged as `system`, and the `downstream` block for script, localise and shot sourcing.
- References: one unified formula catalogue H01–H30 with cross-references to ig-reel, IG1–IG10, Vyral and CreatorSkills ids; an India hook bank (3 per formula across 7 niches, plus Devanagari and English versions); three layers; Reels specifics; language rules; anti-patterns; scoring; critique pass.
- Templates (hook card + downstream, creative pick), checklist, three examples (Hinglish laundry, Hindi real estate, bad hooks annotated).
- At the pick, the gate decision is recorded before the pick is saved as the next `hooks` version (the producer's version check covers what was shown). A plain "ok" / "theek hai" picks the recommended pair.
