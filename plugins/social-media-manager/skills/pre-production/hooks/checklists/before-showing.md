# Before showing: hooks self-check

Run before every save and before showing the creative pick. Record each line in `hooks.data.self_check.items` (`id`, `ok`, `note`). `passed` only if all pass; fix and re-check once, then save with the failing lines noted.

| Id | Check | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| HC1 | **Formula variety** | Per concept (or the quick format): ≥ 5 different formulas among its hooks; the top 3 use at least 2 different formulas; across concepts no duplicate top lines and no formula leading two concepts (`scoring.md`, variety). |
| HC2 | **Visual-led** | ≥ 2 hooks per concept tagged `visual-led`. |
| HC3 | **Proof and real phrases** | ≥ 1 `proof` hook per concept; ≥ 1 `customer-phrase` hook when a real phrase exists (otherwise noted). |
| HC4 | **Three layers** | Every hook has visual, spoken and text; they point at one idea; the muted test passes for the top 3. |
| HC5 | **Text ≤ 6 words** | Every `text` layer is 6 words or fewer and fits the safe zone. |
| HC6 | **Nothing invented** | Every number, price, date, name and claim is from the brief, profile or research; no fake urgency, no unprovable superlative, no fake person. |
| HC7 | **Promise paid** | Each top hook's promise is something its concept delivers, before the halfway mark. |
| HC8 | **Language** | The brief's language and script; spoken lines natural; no too-pure, translated or cringe lines; customer phrases verbatim. |
| HC9 | **Learnings applied** | Every matching learning is reflected in an adjustment and a `boost_note`; no flopped or rejected formula is the recommended hook unless nothing beats it. |
| HC10 | **Critique done** | The 3 weakest per concept were diagnosed and rewritten or replaced; trust anti-patterns are all `replaced`. |
