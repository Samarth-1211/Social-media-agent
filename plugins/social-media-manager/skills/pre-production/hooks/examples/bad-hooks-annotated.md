# Bad: hooks that fail (annotated)

Same laundry job as `good-hinglish-hooks-laundry.md` (SHL-04: ₹89/kg, free pickup above ₹299, Hinglish, working couples and students in Vijay Nagar and Bhawarkua).

| # | Hook (spoken · text · visual) | What kills it | Fixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Namaste dosto! Aaj hum aapko batane wale hain Shubh Laundry ke baare mein." · *Welcome to Shubh Laundry* · logo animation | **slow + brand-first + cringe.** Two seconds of greeting and a logo: the viewer is gone before anything happens. | H26: "Office 10 baje, aur press wala phir nahi aaya." · *Press wala nahi aaya* · 9:40 on the clock |
| 2 | "Kya aap apne vastron ki swachhata ko lekar chintit hain?" · *Swachh vastra, swasth jeevan* · stock photo | **too-pure + vague.** Government-notice Hindi nobody speaks; no concrete noun; a rhyming slogan. | H17: "Poore hafte ke kapde. Ek bag. Dekho." · *Ek bag, poora hafta* · shirts into one bag |
| 3 | "Indore ke 10,000+ happy customers ka bharosa! No.1 laundry!" · *Indore's No.1 laundry* · logo | **invented-number + unprovable-claim.** Nobody gave 10,000; "No.1" needs independent proof. Trust anti-pattern: never in the top 3. | H30 with real facts: "Wash + iron, ₹89 kilo. Pickup free, ₹299 se upar." |
| 4 | "Hurry! Offer sirf aaj tak! Jaldi book karo!" · *Last day!!!* · countdown timer | **fake-urgency + engagement pressure.** The brief has no end date. | No deadline language at all; if the brief had `validity`, H18 with that exact date. |
| 5 | "Gande kapdon mein germs hote hain jo aapke bachchon ko beemar kar sakte hain." · *Germs alert!* · dirty-clothes close-up | **fear + profile violation** (no dirty-clothes close-ups). A health scare to sell ironing. | Show the clean result instead (H13): "Monday ko bag mein. Tuesday ko hanger pe." |
| 6 | "Yeh reel dekh ke aap shock ho jaoge 😱😱🔥" · *You won't believe this* · fast zoom on nothing | **vague tease + cringe.** No category for the answer; emoji rain; nothing the concept pays off. | H03 with a real fact, or a stronger formula: H05 "Pehle poora Sunday press mein jaata tha. Ab ek WhatsApp." |
| 7 | "Sunday ko press karna band karo!" · *STOP IRONING* · iron | **lecture,** and this client's owner rejected "stop doing" openers (learning, active). | H19 permission: "Kapde dhone ke liye Sunday dena zaroori nahi." |

## Patterns to notice

- 1, 2 and 6 fail **attention**: the viewer leaves before the point.
- 3, 4 and 5 fail **trust**: even if they stop the thumb, they cost the client credibility, and break the rules in `anti-patterns.md`.
- 7 is a decent formula that's **wrong for this client**: learnings beat general rules.
- Every fix uses something real: the customers' phrase, the client's price, a real visual.
