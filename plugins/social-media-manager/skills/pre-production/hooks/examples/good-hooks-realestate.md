Brief: Hindi with English moments, **Devanagari** on-screen text (families 30–55), 60 s, YouTube + Instagram; RERA number on every creative; no promises of returns. ## Why this is good

- **The fear is answered, not argued:** delivery anxiety (`PP1`) meets dated monthly footage, the strongest proof a buyer can get (psychology T1).
- **The mandatory becomes a hook:** the RERA number, usually small print, is 1B's whole first frame.
- **Hindi people speak** ("ये रहा", "देखिए कैसे बनी"), not brochure Hindi; Devanagari text for the 30–55 audience; one English line for the NRI audience.
- **No invented facts:** the month count, RERA number and possession date stay `{{needed}}` until the client gives them; no hook is shown to the client with a placeholder in it.
- **No AI where trust lives:** all three openers are real site footage.
