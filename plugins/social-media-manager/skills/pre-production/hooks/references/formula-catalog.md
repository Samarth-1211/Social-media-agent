# Hook formula catalogue (H01–H30)

One list, stable ids. `hooks.data.hooks[].formula_id` and client `learnings[].hook_formula_id` use these ids; never renumber. New formulas get the next free id.

**Cross-reference columns:** `ig-reel` = Jake Schincariol's 26 formulas (`#n`); `IG` = sergebulaev's IG1–IG10 (the market researcher labels `outlier_hooks` with these); `vyral` = Vyral archetype names; `CS` = CreatorSkills' 8 hook types. When research gives an outlier hook as `IG7` or a named formula, map it here first.

Each entry: **pattern** · **use when** · **don't when** · **layers** (V visual / S spoken / T text) · examples (EN, Hinglish; fictional, `{{…}}` = a real fact needed) · **lever** (psychology file).

## Formulas

**H01 Cost confession.** "{Real cost} is what {one mistake} cost {me / a customer}."
- Use: trust with a cold audience; owner-led brands; teach reels. Don't: no real cost exists, or the "cost" is a feeling.
- Layers: V the owner, or the ruined item · S the confession · T the cost.
- EN "That one wrong press cost a customer her favourite saree." · Hi "Ek galat press ne ek customer ki favourite saree bigaad di thi. Tab se yeh rule hai."

**H02 Stop doing X.** "Stop {habit the viewer recognises}. Do {alternative}."
- Use: teach and save-driven reels; habits people really have. Don't: the habit isn't common; the client's learnings say it sounds like a lecture (check `rejected` learnings).
- Layers: V the habit in action · S the command + the alternative · T "STOP {habit}".
- EN "Stop washing silk at home. One cycle is enough to dull it." · Hi "Silk ghar pe dhona band karo. Ek wash mein chamak chali jaati hai."

**H03 Nobody tells you.** "Nobody tells you that {true, uncomfortable thing}."
- Use: expert businesses with a genuinely little-known fact. Don't: the "secret" is common knowledge, or there's no fact behind it (becomes a vague tease).
- Layers: V the owner leaning in, or the detail itself · S the line · T the secret, compressed.
- EN "Nobody tells you the site visit matters more than the brochure." · Hi "Koi nahi batata: flat lene se pehle site pe kya dekhna hai."

**H04 Price gap.** "{This} replaces {expensive thing} for {real price}."
- Use: value offers with a real comparison. Don't: the gap is exaggerated, or it implies a named competitor.
- Layers: V the two side by side · S the swap · T "₹{a} → ₹{b}".
- EN "Our ₹{{X}} pack replaces three salon visits a month." · Hi "Ek ₹{{X}} pack, mahine ki teen salon visits ka kaam."

**H05 Time collapse.** "This used to take {long}. Now it takes {short}."
- Use: convenience services, process improvements. Don't: the ratio isn't believable or isn't measured.
- Layers: V clock or calendar cut · S the before/after time · T "{long} → {short}".
- EN "Ironing used to eat my Sunday. Now it's one WhatsApp." · Hi "Pehle poora Sunday press mein jaata tha. Ab ek WhatsApp."

**H06 The receipt.** "We {did X} for {time}. Here's what actually happened."
- Use: proof with real numbers or footage the client has. Don't: there's no screen, no record, no footage to show.
- Layers: V the record (photos, a register, a dashboard) · S the claim · T "{time}. Real result."
- EN "We filmed this site on the 1st of every month. Watch it rise." · Hi "Har mahine ki 1 tareekh ko site shoot ki. Dekho kaise bani."

**H07 Wrong way, not your fault.** "You're doing {thing} wrong, and it's not your fault. {Fix}."
- Use: teach reels where the viewer feels blamed by the category. Don't: drop the absolution (then it insults).
- Layers: V the common mistake · S the line + fix · T "Galti aapki nahi".
- EN "You're storing your sarees wrong, and nobody told you." · Hi "Sarees galat tarike se rakh rahe ho, aur kisi ne bataya hi nahi."

**H08 Years inside.** "{Real years} doing {thing}. Here's what we never say out loud."
- Use: experienced owners (real years only). Don't: no real years; health or finance advice without real credentials.
- Layers: V the owner at work · S authority + the turn · T "{N} saal, ek sach".
- EN "{{N}} years building homes in Jabalpur. One question buyers forget to ask." · Hi "{{N}} saal se Jabalpur mein ghar bana rahe hain. Ek sawaal sab bhool jaate hain."

**H09 Steal this.** "Steal this {artifact}. {How long it took to get right}." / "How to {result} without {pain}."
- Use: checklists, recipes, routines viewers will save and forward. Don't: the artifact isn't on screen to screenshot.
- Layers: V the artifact, readable · S the offer · T "STEAL THIS" or the result.
- EN "Save this: the 3-question check before you give any dry cleaner your saree." · Hi "Yeh checklist save karo: dry clean dene se pehle 3 sawaal."

**H10 Direct call-out.** "{Specific group / situation}, this is for you."
- Use: narrow, local audiences; footfall; area names. Don't: the group is so broad it calls out nobody ("Indore walon").
- Layers: V someone in that exact situation · S the call-out · T the group or situation.
- EN "Students in Bhawarkua with no time to wash — this is for you." · Hi "Bhawarkua ke hostel walon, kapde dhone ka time nahi? Suno."

**H11 Numbered, with a favourite.** "{N} {things} that {outcome}. Number {k} is the one nobody does."
- Use: teach and curation; N between 3 and 5. Don't: N above 5, or filler items.
- Layers: V counter on screen · S the list promise · T "{N}. #{k} zaroori."
- EN "Three things to check in a flat. The third one, nobody checks." · Hi "Flat mein 3 cheezein check karo. Teesri koi nahi karta."

**H12 Their objection, verbatim.** "\"{Objection exactly as said}.\" Fine. Here's the version that works anyway."
- Use: when research or the profile has a real objection. Don't: the objection is invented.
- Layers: V the objection as a chat bubble · S the quote + the turn · T the quote.
- EN "\"Dry clean is too expensive.\" Fair. Here's what it really costs per wear." · Hi "\"Dry clean bahut mehenga hai.\" Theek hai. Ek baar hisaab dekh lo."

**H13 Before/after on screen.** "{Before}. {After}. {The one thing that changed}."
- Use: anything visual: cleaning, salon, interiors, tailoring. Don't: the after is exaggerated or AI-made; the before shames someone.
- Layers: V the cut between before and after (the hook) · S one line on the lever · T "Before / After" or "Wahi {thing}".
- EN "Same sofa. One deep clean." · Hi "Wahi sofa. Bas ek baar dhulwaya."

**H14 First tries flopped.** "Our first {N} {attempts} failed. {What changed}."
- Use: founder stories, honest brands. Don't: the change sounds like luck.
- Layers: V old failed attempt · S the confession + turn · T "{N} baar fail, phir…".
- EN "Our first three biryani batches got sent back. Then we changed one thing." · Hi "Pehli teen baar biryani wapas aayi. Phir ek cheez badli."

**H15 Customer's question, verbatim.** "\"{Question exactly as asked}\" — we hear this every day."
- Use: a real `customer_words` or research `customer_phrases` question; trust and search. Don't: polish the question into textbook language.
- Layers: V the question as a WhatsApp message · S the quote + "jawab dekh lo" · T the question, short.
- EN "\"Is dry clean safe for silk?\" We get this every day." · Hi "\"Saree ka dry clean safe hai na?\" Roz yeh sawaal aata hai."

**H16 Head to head.** "{A} vs {B}. We tried both. It wasn't close."
- Use: helping a decision (home vs service, rent vs own, plan A vs B). Don't: a named competitor; refusing to pick.
- Layers: V split screen · S the face-off + verdict tease · T "{A} vs {B}".
- EN "Rent vs EMI in Jabalpur: we did the maths for one family." · Hi "Kiraya vs EMI: ek family ka asli hisaab."

**H17 Cold-open demo.** "{Already mid-action} Watch what this does."
- Use: demos, processes, food, salon work; any visible result. Don't: the result takes long to appear (cut to it).
- Layers: V hands already doing it, motion at frame 1 · S a pointer line · T "Dekho" or the result.
- EN "Watch this stain on a white kurta." · Hi "Safed kurte pe yeh daag. Ab dekho."

**H18 Real deadline / timely.** "{Real change or date}. Do {action} before then."
- Use: real offer end dates (`offer_detail.validity`), festivals, seasons, admissions. Don't: any deadline that isn't real; "only today" pressure.
- Layers: V the calendar or the occasion · S the date + action · T "{date} tak".
- EN "Diwali guests arrive in a week. Curtains first." · Hi "Diwali ke mehmaan ek hafte mein. Curtain pehle."

**H19 Permission.** "You don't need {the thing everyone says}. {What you do need}."
- Use: relief for an overwhelmed audience (first-time buyers, students). Don't: removing the blocker and offering nothing.
- Layers: V relaxed person · S the permission + the replacement · T "Zaroori nahi".
- EN "You don't need a big budget for a wedding look. You need the right fit." · Hi "Shaadi ke look ke liye bada budget zaroori nahi. Sahi fitting zaroori hai."

**H20 Mid-sentence story.** "…and that's when {the turn}."
- Use: customer and founder stories; brand films. Don't: never filling in the setup by second 8.
- Layers: V the turning moment · S the mid-sentence line · T the turn, short.
- EN "…and that's when she said the wedding was tomorrow." · Hi "…aur tab unhone bataya, shaadi kal hai."

**H21 Contrarian truth.** "Everyone says {X}. Actually {Y}."
- Use: opinion reels with a defensible, provable position. Don't: the payoff can't defend it; it mocks a competitor.
- Layers: V the expected thing, then a cut · S the claim · T the flipped line.
- EN "Everyone says buy ready-made for Diwali. A tailored kurta costs less than you think." · Hi "Sab kehte hain ready-made le lo. Silwana sasta padta hai, hisaab dekho."

**H22 Sourced statistic.** "{Real statistic} — {source}."
- Use: education, real estate, health, only with a named source the viewer can check. Don't: any unsourced number.
- Layers: V the number large · S the stat + why it matters · T "{N}%: {source}".
- EN "{{Statistic}} — {{source, year}}. Here's what that means for you." · Hi "{{Stat}}, {{source}} ke hisaab se. Iska matlab yeh hai."

**H23 The reveal.** "This is the new {thing}."
- Use: launches, new menus, new branches, a new collection. Don't: a reveal with no stake (then it's an ad).
- Layers: V the object uncovered at frame 1 · S what it changes · T "Naya {thing}".
- EN "This is our new Napier Town branch, and parking is finally easy." · Hi "Yeh hai humari nayi Napier Town branch. Parking ki tension khatam."

**H24 Customer's result.** "{Real customer} {got real outcome} in {real time}."
- Use: real, consenting customers; coaching, clinics, services. Don't: anonymous miracles; AI or actor "customers"; health claims as guarantees.
- Layers: V the real customer · S their result · T the result.
- EN "{{Name}} moved her family out of a rented flat in eight months." · Hi "{{Name}} ji ne aath mahine mein kiraye ka ghar chhoda."

**H25 Provable superlative.** "The {fastest / only / first} {thing} in {place} is {answer}."
- Use: only when it's provable and current (a real "only", a real "first"). Don't: "No.1", "best", "cheapest" without independent proof.
- Layers: V the thing · S the claim + the proof · T the claim.
- EN "The only laundry in Vijay Nagar with same-day silk care." (if true) · Hi "Vijay Nagar mein same-day silk care sirf yahan." (agar sach hai)

**H26 Relatable moment.** "{A specific everyday moment the viewer has lived}."
- Use: everyday pain, humour, awareness; the strongest Indian SMB opener when it's specific. Don't: a vague mood ("life is busy").
- Layers: V the moment as a scene · S a line from inside it · T the moment, short.
- EN "Guests at the door, and the curtains are from last Diwali." · Hi "Mehmaan aa gaye, aur curtain abhi bhi pichhli Diwali wale."

**H27 Withhold.** "{Show the result}. I'm not telling you how… yet."
- Use: transformations, food, salon work, where the reveal is the value. Don't: holding it past the halfway mark; never revealing.
- Layers: V the result · S the tease · T "Kaise? Dekhte raho."
- EN "This blouse was a size too big an hour ago." · Hi "Yeh blouse ek ghante pehle ek size bada tha."

**H28 How we do it.** "Here's exactly what happens to your {thing} inside."
- Use: trust-heavy services (psychology T1); first-person proof ("How I…" beats "How to…"). Don't: invented steps; a messy space.
- Layers: V the inside: counter, kitchen, site · S the promise · T "Andar kya hota hai?"
- EN "Here's what happens to your saree the minute it reaches our counter." · Hi "Aapki saree counter pe aate hi pehle yeh hota hai."

**H29 Myth vs fact.** "Myth: {belief}. Fact: {truth}."
- Use: teach and growth reels; experts. Don't: the "fact" isn't solid; it debunks a named competitor.
- Layers: V "MYTH" card or the believer · S myth + the turn · T "Myth: {belief}".
- EN "Myth: dry cleaning fades colours. Fact: wrong washing does." · Hi "Myth: dry clean se rang chala jaata hai. Sach: galat dhulai se jaata hai."

**H30 Price-first offer card.** "{Offer + real price/discount} — {what it's for}, {until when}."
- Use: offer reels, Tier 2/3 deal-aware buyers (psychology T5), clients whose learnings say "price pehle dikhao". Don't: no real price or offer in the brief; a fake "original" price.
- Layers: V the offer card over the real product or place, frame 1 · S the offer in one breath · T the offer.
- EN "Curtain + sofa cleaning, 20% off, till 25 Nov." · Hi "Diwali se pehle curtain aur sofa, 20% off, 25 Nov tak."

## Choosing formulas for a concept

| Concept angle type | Strong formulas |
|---|---|
| proof | H06, H13, H17, H24, H28, H15 |
| teach | H02, H07, H09, H11, H29, H03 |
| opinion | H21, H19, H16, H12 |
| story | H20, H26, H14, H01 |
| offer | H30, H18, H04, H05, H10 |

Mix across rows to reach 5+ formulas; always include at least two visual-led ones.
