# India hook bank

Example hooks per formula (`formula-catalog.md`) across Indian small-business niches. **Fictional businesses; `{{…}}` marks a real fact the client must supply.** Use them to calibrate tone and specificity; never copy one into a job without making it this client's own (their place, their phrase, their price).

Niches: 🧺 laundry / home services · 🏠 real estate · 👗 clothing · 🍲 food / restaurant · 📚 coaching / education · 💇 clinic / salon · ✈️ travel. Language: Roman Hinglish unless marked (Hi = Hindi, Devanagari; En = English).

Format: **spoken** · *on-screen text (≤ 6 words)* · first frame.
