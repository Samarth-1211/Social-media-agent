# Reels specifics

What changes when the hook is for Instagram Reels (the main surface for our clients; YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status follow the same rules unless noted).

## The three-second cliff

Drop-off in the first three seconds tells the platform the hook failed, and the reel stops being shown to new people. Everything in the opening serves getting past second 3:
- the first frame does real work (no logo, no black frame, no slow zoom, no "hi guys");
- the first spoken word is the most interesting one;
- a 15-second reel and a 60-second reel die at the same cliff.

## On-screen text timing

- The hook text is up **at frame 1** and holds **2–3 seconds**.
- Later beats get short labels (3–4 words) only when they help; text is punctuation, not a running caption track.
- The payoff line gets its own clean text beat.
- The CTA gets its own end frame and text (`cta_detail.action`), not piled onto the payoff.
- Body captions (subtitles) are burned in for the whole reel; the hook text is separate and bigger.

## Safe zones (9:16, 1080 × 1920)

Instagram's interface covers the edges: the caption and audio strip at the bottom, the account row, the action rail on the right.
- Keep text and key action **between y = 230 and y = 1440**, and **out of the right-hand 230 px**.
- Put the hook text in the **upper two-thirds**: the grid shows reels cropped to 3:4, so the first frame must still read when the top and bottom are cut.
- High contrast: bold, large, a solid or shadowed box on busy footage (shop interiors in sunlight are busy).
- *Re-check against Instagram's current layout each season; these pixel values come from the `ig-reel` guidance, not from Meta.*

## The first frame is also the cover

The first frame is what people see in the Reels tab, on the grid and in a shared WhatsApp preview. Treat it as a poster: one face, one object or one contrast, plus the hook text. Avoid other apps' watermarks or interface chrome in the frame (reposts get less reach).

## Payoff preview: built to be sent

On Reels, a send (a DM or WhatsApp forward) is worth more than a like. A good hook previews a payoff someone would send to one specific person: "the friend who's buying a flat", "the didi whose wedding is next month", "the family group". If you can name who it gets forwarded to, the hook is working. For our audience (psychology T4), that forward usually goes to WhatsApp.

## Length and pace

- Hooks resolve by second 3; the stake follows by second 7.
- Change the frame every beat; a static shot longer than about 4 seconds is where people leave.
- Tier 3 and older audiences: slightly slower cuts and fully clear text, but the hook window doesn't get longer.

## Audio

Original voice for talking heads, demos and owners. Trending audio only if the brand's account type can use it and it fits the energy; never build a hook that only works with a sound the client can't license.
