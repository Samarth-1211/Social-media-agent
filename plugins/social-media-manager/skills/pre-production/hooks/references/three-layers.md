# The three layers of a hook

Every hook runs three layers at once in the first 1.5–2 seconds. All three are required in `layers`, and all three must point at **one** idea.

| Layer | Field | What it is | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visual** | `visual` | What's on screen in the first 1.5 s | Shows the payoff, the conflict or the stake. Motion at frame 1. Reads on a paused phone. No logo, black frame, slow zoom or fade-in. |
| **Spoken** | `spoken` | The first line said | 5–12 words, lands within about 2 s. The most interesting word comes first. One claim, said once. No "Namaste dosto", no "aaj hum baat karenge". |
| **On-screen text** | `text` | The words burned in on frame 1 | **≤ 6 words** (3–5 is better). Sharpens the spoken line, doesn't repeat it. Readable with the sound off. Inside the safe zone (`reels-specifics.md`). |
| Audio (optional) | `audio` | The timing cue | A beat drop on the first cut, a sudden silence before the reveal, a natural sound (a shutter, a tawa sizzle). Note it only when it matters. |

## The sound-off rule

Many people watch muted first. **Cover the sound:** do the visual and the text alone make the viewer stop and understand what this is about? If not, the hook fails, however good the spoken line is.

Then the two other covers:
- **Cover the text:** does the visual still pull?
- **Look away:** does the spoken line alone pull the viewer back?

All three should pass. A hook that only works with audio wastes the reach.

## How to write the three together

1. **Spoken first.** Easiest to test aloud. Cut until it's under 2 seconds.
2. **Visual next:** the frame that lands *before* the voice. If the voice has to explain what we're looking at, the visual isn't working.
3. **Text last, as a sharpener:** drop verbs and fillers, keep the number, the contrast or the stake. Spoken "Diwali se pehle curtain aur sofa, 20% off, 25 Nov tak" → text "Curtain + sofa: 20% off".
4. **Alignment check:** all three about the same thing? A clever line over an unrelated pretty shot reads as confused.

## Visual-led hooks

A hook is **visual-led** (`tags: visual-led`) when the first frame alone does the hooking: a before/after cut (H13), a hand already doing the thing (H17), an object revealed (H23), a result with the "how" held back (H27), an offer card on the real product (H30), a lived moment filmed as a scene (H26, H20). Each concept needs at least two.

Strong Indian SMB first frames: the hand-over at the counter, the steam off a tawa, the tag pinned on a saree, the site board at dawn, the WhatsApp message on a phone, the empty hanger with tomorrow's date, the stain on a white kurta.
