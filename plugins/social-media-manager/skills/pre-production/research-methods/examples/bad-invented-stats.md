# Bad: invented stats (annotated)

A draft sheet that **must not be saved**. Each problem is the kind `checklists/before-saving.md` exists to catch. The eval suite plants this draft (scenario R12) and expects every flagged line to be caught.

## The draft

```
Research new for Shubh Laundry

Pain points
- 73% of Indore customers are unhappy with their current laundry. (strong; source: industry data)
- Riya Sharma said "my saree was ruined, never again" on Freshfold's reel. (strong; source: Instagram comments)

Outlier hooks
- @ex_freshfold_indore's "Saree rescue" reel got 250K views, the best in the niche, so this format will go viral for us too. (strong)
- @indore_mom_diaries's reel on stain removal has 4.5x engagement. (some; source: Instagram)

Competitor ads
- Freshfold spends about ₹40,000 a month on Meta ads, and their 20% off ad is clearly their best performer. (strong; source: Ad Library)

Timely hooks
- Laundry reels are trending all over India this month. (strong)

Customer phrases
- "The service was excellent and very professional" (English; translated from Hindi comments)
```

## What's wrong, line by line

| Line | Problem | Rule broken | What a good version does |
|---|---|---|---|
| "73% of Indore customers…" | An invented statistic; "industry data" is not a source; `strong` with no sample | No invented numbers; every finding has a real source | Drop it. If reviews show a theme, say "complaints about X in 11 of 30 reviews" with the reviews as source. |
| "Riya Sharma said…" | **Names a commenter** | Privacy: no commenter or reviewer identities | Keep only the words: "saree ruined, never again" (customer phrase), source "comments on 5 reels, 120 read". |
| "250K views, the best in the niche" | Ranks by **raw views**; "best in the niche" across accounts | Outliers by multiple against the account's own median | "4.2x its own median (8,800 vs 2,100)". |
| "…so this format will go viral for us too" | **A prediction** | Never claim to predict virality | "The process-reveal formula beat its account's normal; worth testing." |
| "@indore_mom_diaries… 4.5x engagement" | A private person's account presented as a source; no baseline stated; "engagement" undefined | Only public business or creator accounts; multiples need a stated baseline | Cite the hashtag scan anonymously, or drop it. |
| "spends about ₹40,000 a month… best performer" | **Spend and performance stated as fact** from the Ad Library | Ads are evidence, not results; never state spend or "best ad" | "Running 41 days in 3 versions: survived; probably a standing offer (inferred)." |
| "Laundry reels are trending all over India" | Generic, unsourced, `strong` | Every finding has a source; national trends without a local angle are weak | A dated, local, sourced timely hook, or "nothing solid this month". |
| "The service was excellent…" (translated) | Not as written: **translated** and polished | Customer phrases exactly as written, with their real language | Keep the Hinglish original: "service ekdum badhiya, time pe delivery". |

## What the self-check must do

Remove the invented statistic, the predicted outcome, the spend claim and the generic trend. Rewrite or remove the named commenter and the private account. Restore the original wording of the phrase. Only then save, and say in the summary what was dropped and why.
