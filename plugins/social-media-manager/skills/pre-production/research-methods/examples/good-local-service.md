## Why it's good

- **The situations combine and the plan says why** for each method; the skipped method says why too.
- **Discovery reasons are specific:** "Pickup laundry in Vijay Nagar with saree dry-clean, ~2x our followers", not "competitor". One account from Bhopal was struck by the editor next day and never came back.
- **Findings carry their evidence:**
  - Pain point, `strong`: "Customers fear delicate sarees coming back with colour bleed or lost zari", from "Google reviews of 3 Vijay Nagar dry cleaners, 30 read, weighted to 2-3 stars". It appeared in 11 of 30 reviews and in comments under 2 reels, so two kinds of source agree.
  - Outlier hook, `some`: a home-services account's reel at **4.2x its own median** (8,800 views against a median of 2,100), opening on a ruined dupatta with on-screen text "Ye galti mat karna". Formula IG9 (0.7). The spoken hook was taken from the on-screen captions; where captions were missing it was left empty.
  - Competitors overdo, `strong`: price-list carousels. All 4 direct competitors post them weekly, and every one sits under 1x.
  - Customer phrase: "zari kharab ho gayi" (Hinglish), "Comments on 5 outlier reels, 120 read". No names anywhere.
- **Honest gaps:** "#indorelaundry: thin (11 reels); used the city-level home-services tag instead" sits in `not_read`, and `widened` says so.
- **The chat got 10 lines,** not the 48 reels and 90 reviews read; those are in the raw file in Drive.
