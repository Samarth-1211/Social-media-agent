## Why this is good

- **The proof is the film.** Delivery anxiety (the buyer's real fear) is answered with dated footage and the RERA number on screen, not with adjectives.
- **The mandatory works for the story:** the RERA board is a scene, and the end card carries the number and the MP RERA website.
- **Every placeholder is visible** to the editor (possession month, the first-month label) and gets an unlock. Nothing is guessed to make the film feel complete.
- **Slower Hindi pace for 30–55 viewers,** with short lines for a director who isn't a voice artist.
- **One CTA** (the sample-flat visit, as on the approved NRH-01 job); no WhatsApp ask competing with it.
