# Caption and CTA

The text beside the video. The video already carries the hook, so the caption's job is the ask, the context that makes the ask easy, and the words people search for.

## 1. One CTA

- **Exactly one ask per video**, from the brief's `cta_detail` (`action`, `channel`). For local businesses it's usually WhatsApp or a call; for real estate, a site or sample-flat visit.
- **Said once and shown once:** the voiceover says it (`said_in_scene`), a card shows it (`shown_in_scene`), and the caption repeats it.
- **Make it easy:** name the next step and remove a worry ("WhatsApp karo, pickup hum karenge"). A keyword ask uses one typeable word, also said in the video ("WhatsApp pe 'CURTAIN' bhejo").
- **No bait:** never "like and share", "follow for more", "tag a friend", "comment YES". On an awareness reel with no offer, the one ask may be save or send, tied to a reason ("Diwali se pehle wali list: save kar lo").
- **Never a phone number in a spoken line or the caption** unless the brief puts it there; `cta_detail.target` is a label ("shop WhatsApp on the end card").

## 2. Caption shape

```
first_line   ≤ 125 characters, stands alone before "...more": the offer or the payoff,
             in the viewer's words, with the topic word. Never a greeting, a hashtag or an emoji first.
body         1–3 short lines: the condition, the date, where, how. One idea.
the ask      the same CTA as the video.
hashtags     3–5, niche and local, on the last line.
```

- **Length:** usually 150–300 characters in total. Longer only for a story caption the brief asks for.
- **Search words:** the phrase a customer would type ("sofa cleaning Indore", "2BHK Napier Town"), once, early, in a normal sentence.
- **Hashtags (3–5):** one city or area tag (#Indore, #VijayNagar), one or two service tags (#SofaCleaning), one occasion tag if real (#DiwaliCleaning), or a brand tag the client already uses. Never #viral, #fyp, #explore, #trending, or 30-tag walls. YouTube Shorts: the tags go in the description, plus #Shorts.
- **Emoji:** 0–2, each with a job (📍 before an area, 🪔 for Diwali). No emoji rows.
- **Pinned comment (optional):** what didn't fit (timings, area list, "kaise book karein") or the answer to the obvious objection. It must carry only true facts; no invented timings.

## 3. Platform differences

| Platform | Visible before the cut | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels / Facebook Reels | ~125 characters | Captions are searchable: words beat hashtags. |
| YouTube Shorts | Title ~40 characters on the tile; description ~100–125 | Title = the hook in words; `#Shorts` in the description. |
| Meta ad | Primary text ~125 characters, headline ~40 | The ad's CTA button replaces the spoken ask's channel: match them (WhatsApp → "Send WhatsApp message"). |
| WhatsApp Status | No caption read | Everything that matters is on the cards. |
